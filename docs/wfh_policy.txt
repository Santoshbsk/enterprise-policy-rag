WORK FROM HOME POLICY

Policy ID: HR-WFH-001
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Department: Human Resources

1. Purpose

The Work From Home policy allows eligible employees to work remotely while maintaining productivity, security, and collaboration.

2. Eligibility

Employees who have completed their probation period may request work from home.

Eligibility may vary depending on job responsibilities, client requirements, security restrictions, and business needs.

Employees working on projects that require physical presence may not be eligible for regular work from home.

3. Work From Home Allowance

Eligible employees may work from home up to 2 days per week.

Employees must work from the location registered with the company unless an alternative location has been approved.

4. Manager Approval

Employees must obtain manager approval before working from home.

Managers may deny work-from-home requests based on business requirements, customer commitments, team availability, or operational needs.

5. Working Hours

Employees working from home must follow their assigned working hours.

Employees must remain available through approved communication tools during working hours.

6. Meetings

Employees working remotely are expected to attend all required meetings.

Employees should have a reliable internet connection and access to required collaboration tools.

7. Information Security

Company confidential information must not be accessed from unsecured public computers.

Employees must use company-approved devices and security tools when working remotely.

Employees must not share company credentials with other individuals.

8. Equipment

The company may provide laptops and other equipment required for remote work.

Employees are responsible for taking reasonable care of company equipment.

9. Productivity

Employees working from home are expected to maintain the same productivity and performance standards applicable when working from the office.

10. Client Requirements

Client-specific work-from-office requirements take priority over this general Work From Home policy.

11. Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy require approval from the employee's manager and HR.

12. Policy Changes

The company may modify or suspend work-from-home arrangements based on business requirements.
