COMPANY BUSINESS TRAVEL POLICY

Policy ID: FIN-TRAVEL-001
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Department: Finance and Administration

1. Purpose

This policy defines the rules for business-related travel undertaken by employees.

2. Travel Approval

All business travel must be approved by the employee's reporting manager before booking.

International travel additionally requires approval from the department head.

Employees should submit travel requests at least 7 calendar days before the planned departure date.

3. Flights

Employees may book economy-class flights for domestic business travel.

Employees may book economy-class flights for international travel when the flight duration is less than 6 hours.

For international flights exceeding 6 hours, employees at manager level and above may book premium economy.

Business-class travel requires approval from the department head.

4. Hotels

Employees may stay at company-approved hotels.

The maximum hotel reimbursement is:

Domestic travel:
INR 6,000 per night.

International travel:
USD 150 per night.

Exceptions require prior approval from the department head.

5. Local Transportation

Employees may claim reasonable expenses for taxis, ride-sharing services, airport transfers, and public transportation used for business purposes.

Receipts should be retained wherever available.

6. Meals

Employees may claim reasonable meal expenses during approved business travel.

The daily meal allowance for domestic travel is INR 1,500.

The daily meal allowance for international travel is USD 50.

7. Travel Expenses

Employees must submit travel expense claims within 10 working days after returning from a business trip.

Expense claims must include receipts for individual expenses above INR 1,000.

8. Personal Travel

Personal expenses are not reimbursable.

If an employee extends a business trip for personal reasons, the additional hotel, transportation, and meal costs must be paid by the employee.

9. Travel Cancellation

Employees should cancel unused travel bookings as soon as possible.

Any cancellation charges caused by personal reasons may not be reimbursed.

10. Travel Insurance

The company provides travel insurance for approved international business trips.

Employees should contact HR or the travel desk for insurance-related assistance.
