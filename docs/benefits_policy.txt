EMPLOYEE BENEFITS POLICY

Policy ID: HR-BENEFITS-001
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Department: Human Resources

1. Health Insurance

Eligible full-time employees are covered under the company's group health insurance plan.

The standard employee coverage limit is INR 500,000 per policy year.

Employees may add eligible dependents subject to the terms of the insurance plan.

2. Life Insurance

Eligible employees receive group life insurance coverage equal to three times their annual base salary.

The benefit is subject to the terms and conditions of the company's insurance provider.

3. Accidental Insurance

Employees are covered under the company's group personal accident insurance program.

The standard accidental coverage is INR 2,000,000.

4. Provident Fund

Eligible employees participate in the applicable provident fund program.

Employee and employer contributions are calculated according to applicable company policy and statutory requirements.

5. Gratuity

Eligible employees may receive gratuity according to applicable statutory requirements and company policy.

6. Meal Benefits

Employees may receive meal benefits through the company's designated meal benefit program.

The applicable monthly benefit is determined by company policy and may be revised periodically.

7. Wellness Benefits

Employees may participate in company wellness programs.

Wellness programs may include health assessments, fitness initiatives, counseling resources, and wellness activities.

8. Learning and Development

Employees may receive access to company-sponsored learning programs, technical training, certifications, and professional development resources.

Training eligibility may depend on role, business requirements, and manager approval.

9. Internet Reimbursement

Eligible employees working under approved remote-work arrangements may receive internet reimbursement.

The maximum reimbursement is INR 1,000 per month.

Employees must submit valid documentation according to the expense reimbursement process.

10. Benefit Changes

The company reserves the right to modify benefit plans based on business requirements, provider changes, or applicable regulations.

11. Authoritative Information

Employees should contact HR or access the Employee Benefits Portal for their current individual benefit eligibility and coverage details.
