COMPANY LEAVE POLICY

Policy ID: HR-LEAVE-001
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Department: Human Resources

1. Annual Leave

All full-time employees are entitled to 20 days of annual leave per calendar year.

Annual leave is credited at the beginning of each calendar year.

Employees joining during the year receive annual leave on a prorated basis.

Employees should submit annual leave requests at least 5 working days before the intended start date.

All annual leave requests require approval from the employee's reporting manager.

2. Sick Leave

Employees are entitled to 12 days of sick leave per calendar year.

Employees should inform their manager as soon as possible when they are unable to work due to illness.

A medical certificate may be required for sick leave exceeding 3 consecutive working days.

3. Casual Leave

Employees are entitled to 6 days of casual leave per calendar year.

Casual leave may be used for personal activities, appointments, or other short-term requirements.

Casual leave should normally be requested at least 2 working days in advance.

4. Carry Forward

Employees may carry forward up to 10 unused annual leave days to the following calendar year.

Unused casual leave cannot be carried forward.

Unused sick leave cannot be carried forward.

5. Leave During Notice Period

Employees serving their notice period must obtain manager and HR approval before taking planned leave.

6. Public Holidays

Company-declared public holidays are not deducted from an employee's annual leave balance.

7. Emergency Leave

Employees may request emergency leave when unexpected personal or family circumstances require immediate absence.

Emergency leave is subject to manager approval and applicable leave balance.

8. Leave Balance

Employees can check their current leave balance through the Employee Self-Service portal.

The HR system is the authoritative source for an employee's actual leave balance.
